You are analyzing a proposed framework called Recursive Adaptive Dynamics (RAD), evolved from a research program studying how systems expand and preserve future adaptive possibility.

The central question is:

"What structural properties allow a system to remain open to environmental correction while recursively improving its ability to generate future improvements?"

Do not interpret this as a theory of intelligence, consciousness, or optimization. Treat it as a causal framework about adaptive systems.

==================================================
CORE INVARIANT
==================================================

The fundamental invariant is:

Open-ended adaptive capacity exists when reality retains causal access to the mechanisms that determine future change.

Compressed:

C_open ≡ ∂C_rev / ∂E* > 0

where:

E* = consequence-bearing environmental information
C_rev = the mechanism controlling how future transformations are revised

Meaning:

A system is not open because it changes.
A system is open because reality can change how it changes.

Distinction:

Change of system:
X_t → X_(t+1)

Change of representation:
K_t → K_(t+1)

Change of generator:
G_t → G_(t+1)

Change of transformation-selection mechanism:
C_rev,t → C_rev,t+1

The deepest transition is the last one.
